# IEEE quick reference

## In-text citations

- Numbered in square brackets in **order of first appearance**: [1], [2]… A source keeps its number every time it is cited again.
- Place the number inside the sentence, before punctuation: "…as defined by NIST [3]."
- Several sources: [2], [5] or a range [2]–[4].
- Specific part: [3, p. 12], [3, Sec. 2.1], [3, Fig. 4].
- The number can act as a noun: "In [4], the authors propose…".
- In capture notes, line 1 names the number: `Hyperlink goes on the citation [3] (Sec. 2.2)`.

## Reference list

Title "References". Listed by number, not alphabetically. Initials before surnames; up to six authors, then the first author followed by "et al.". Article titles in quotes, journal/book/standard titles in italics. Online sources end with `[Online]. Available: URL` and, for pages that change, `Accessed: Mon. DD, YYYY.`

## Formats by source type

**Journal article**
[1] I. Weber and M. Staples, "Programmable money: Next-generation blockchain-based conditional payments," *Digital Finance*, vol. 4, no. 2, pp. 109–125, 2022, doi: 10.1007/s42521-022-00059-5.

**Conference paper**
[2] A. Author and B. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. Conf. Name*, City, Country, 2024, pp. 1–6, doi: …

**Standard**
[3] *IEEE Standard for General Process of Cryptocurrency Payment*, IEEE Std 2143.1-2020, 2020.
[4] *Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technologies — Vocabulary*, ISO 22739:2024, 2024.

**Technical report / government document**
[5] L. Lesavre, P. Varin, and D. Yaga, "Blockchain networks: Token design and management overview," Nat. Inst. Standards Technol., Gaithersburg, MD, USA, NISTIR 8301, 2021. [Online]. Available: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8301

**Patent**
[6] S. J. Hurry and A. Pierre, "Digital fiat currency," U.S. Patent 11 507 929 B2, Nov. 22, 2022.

**Website / web page**
[7] A. Lee, "What is programmable money?," FEDS Notes, Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Jun. 23, 2021. [Online]. Available: https://www.federalreserve.gov/...

**Press release**
[8] Gartner, "Gartner unveils top emerging technologies to support autonomous business," Press release, Sep. 10, 2025. [Online]. Available: https://www.gartner.com/...

**Live data / dashboard**
[9] Stablecoin Beat, "Stablecoin market cap tracker." [Online]. Available: https://stablecoinbeat.com/tracker/ (accessed Oct. 24, 2026).

**Figure caption**
Fig. 2. Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies, 2025 [8].
