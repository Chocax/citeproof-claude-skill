# APA 7 quick reference

Use Spanish conventions when the document is in Spanish (`y`, `s.f.`, `et al.`, dates as "10 de septiembre") and English conventions otherwise (`&`, `n.d.`, "September 10").

## In-text citations

| Case | Parenthetical | Narrative |
|---|---|---|
| One author | (Lee, 2021) | Lee (2021) |
| Two authors | (Weber y Staples, 2022) / (Weber & Staples, 2022) | Weber y Staples (2022) |
| Three or more | (Lesavre et al., 2021) | Lesavre et al. (2021) |
| Organization | (Gartner, 2025) | Gartner (2025) |
| Organization with abbreviation | first: (Bank for International Settlements [BIS], 2023); then (BIS, 2023) | |
| No date | (Circle, s.f.) / (Circle, n.d.) | |
| Same author, same year | (Gartner, 2025a), (Gartner, 2025b) — letters assigned by title order in the reference list | |
| Same author, several no-date works | (NIST, s.f.-a), (NIST, s.f.-b) | |
| Direct quote | add page or paragraph: (Lee, 2021, párr. 3) / (Lee, 2021, p. 4) | |
| Several works | (ISO, 2019; Lee, 2021) — alphabetical, separated by semicolons | |

## Reference list

- Alphabetical by first author; works by the same author ordered by year (no-date first), then a/b/c.
- Hanging indent 0.5 in (1.27 cm), double-spaced unless the user's format says otherwise.
- Italicize the title of standalone works (reports, web pages, standards, books) and the journal name + volume for articles.
- Include the URL or DOI as a clickable hyperlink. No "Retrieved from" unless the content changes over time (then "Consultado el 24 de octubre de 2026, de URL").

## Formats by source type

**Journal article**
Weber, I., & Staples, M. (2022). Programmable money: Next-generation blockchain-based conditional payments. *Digital Finance, 4*(2), 109–125. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42521-022-00059-5

**Web page / article on a site**
Lee, A. (2021, June 23). *What is programmable money?* FEDS Notes. Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. https://www.federalreserve.gov/...

**Report or working paper**
Lavayssière, X., & Zhang, N. (2024). *Programmability in payment and settlement* (IMF Working Paper No. 2024/177). International Monetary Fund. https://...

**Government / standards document with number**
Lesavre, L., Varin, P., & Yaga, D. (2021). *Blockchain networks: Token design and management overview* (NISTIR 8301). National Institute of Standards and Technology. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8301

**Standard (ISO, IEEE)**
International Organization for Standardization. (2024). *Blockchain and distributed ledger technologies — Vocabulary* (ISO Standard No. 22739:2024). https://www.iso.org/standard/82208.html
IEEE. (2020). *IEEE standard for general process of cryptocurrency payment* (IEEE Std 2143.1-2020). https://standards.ieee.org/...

**Patent**
Hurry, S. J., & Pierre, A. (2022). *Digital fiat currency* (U.S. Patent No. 11,507,929 B2). U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. https://patents.google.com/patent/US11507929
(When inventors are unknown, the assignee can be the author.)

**Press release**
Gartner. (2025, September 10). *Gartner unveils top emerging technologies to support autonomous business* [Press release]. https://www.gartner.com/...

**Live data / dashboard**
Stablecoin Beat. (2026). *Stablecoin market cap tracker*. Retrieved October 24, 2026, from https://stablecoinbeat.com/tracker/

**Figure reproduced from a source** (caption)
*Figura 2.* Hype Cycle for Emerging Technologies, 2025. Fuente: Gartner (2025a).
