# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-24
- First release.
- APA 7 and IEEE citation styles.
- Baseline source policy (verifiable, veridical, recognizable) plus user-defined source rules.
- Two evidence modes: automatic screenshots (`scripts/capture.py`) and manual capture notes with clickable links.
- `.docx` helpers (`scripts/docx_notes.js`) that produce real hyperlinks for Ctrl+click.
